The fine print

Questions people ask.

How accurate are the numbers?

They're solid estimates. We multiply each appliance's wattage by how long you run it, then by your tariff. Your real bill also depends on the weather, the machine's age, and your tariff plan — so treat it as a reliable guide.

What does Standard actually do?

Standard shows you, month by month, which appliances are using the most electricity. It's the diagnosis. Premium is the treatment — a personal plan and savings tracking that starts working on day one.

What tariff should I enter?

Look at your bill for the price per kWh (usually 1–3 kr in Sweden including taxes and grid fees). We start at 2,50 kr/kWh, and you can change it anytime.

Is this a real subscription?

This is a demo. Joining and upgrading are simulated in your browser and nothing is charged. A production version would plug into a real payment provider.
