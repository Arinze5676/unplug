# 1-minute Q&A prep

**Q: Is this real or just a mockup?**
> It's a working front-end demo — the calculator, tiers, and savings page all run in the browser. A production version adds live meter data and a payment provider.

**Q: Where do the numbers come from?**
> We multiply each appliance's wattage by hours and your tariff (default 2.5 kr/kWh, editable). Savings estimates are based on published feedback and behaviour studies.

**Q: How do you get real usage data?**
> The MVP is manual entry, which works for everyone today. Later we plug into smart-meter APIs the way Greenely and others do.

**Q: Why would a student pay 65 kr to save 65 kr?**
> Because the free tier already saves them money; Premium saves *more* and does the work for them — plus it pays for itself in the first cold month if they have any electric heating.
